# Research and Dependency Gate

At every phase and material phase update:
1. Research current primary repositories/documentation and relevant current accounting/church-accounting applications.
2. Compare at least one general accounting workflow and one church/nonprofit-specific workflow when relevant.
3. Record date, source, insight, ChurchBooks decision, and license/dependency impact in `docs/research/RESEARCH-REGISTER.md`.
4. Adopt concepts, not copied proprietary UI/source.
5. Open-source code may only be reused when its license and architectural fit are explicitly acceptable.
6. Avoid adding packages merely because they are popular.
7. Recheck current versions/security before pinning or upgrading packages.
