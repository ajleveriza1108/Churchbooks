# Phase Research Skill

For every phase or material update:
1. Search current primary repos/docs for the technical stack and candidate packages.
2. Search current official product/help material for QuickBooks/Xero or another general accounting product when relevant.
3. Search current official church/nonprofit accounting products/support material when relevant.
4. Record sources and dates in `docs/research/RESEARCH-REGISTER.md`.
5. For each finding mark: Adopt, Adapt, Defer, or Reject. Explain why.
6. Check license/version/security before adding a dependency.
7. Do not copy proprietary code/UI/branding.
8. Turn durable lessons into `.clinerules`, `AGENTS.md`, tests, or a focused skill so the research compounds across phases.
